What is Expense Tracker?

Expense Tracker is a full-stack web application designed specifically for students and young professionals in India. It helps you understand where your money goes, stay within budget, and build smarter financial habits.

Unlike complex finance tools, Expense Tracker is lightweight, beautiful, and built around real student needs — from splitting mess bills to tracking travel budgets with automatic INR conversion.

"A rupee saved is a rupee earned." — The philosophy behind every feature

Everything You Need

Packed with features built for real student life

📊

Smart Dashboard

Live charts showing monthly spending, category breakdown, budget utilisation, and day-of-week patterns — all at a glance.

🔄

Multi-Currency

Record expenses in INR, USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AED, or SGD. All amounts auto-convert to INR for unified tracking.

🤝

Bill Splitting

Split trips, outings, or dinners equally, by percentage, or custom amounts. Track who's paid and who still owes.

🍽️

Mess Bill Manager

Track monthly mess/hostel bills, mark payments, and keep a record of all your dining history in one place.

🎯

Savings Goals

Set savings targets with deadlines, track progress visually, and get motivated to reach your financial milestones.

🤖

AI / ML Insights

Powered by DistilBERT and scikit-learn — auto-category prediction, anomaly detection, spend forecasting, and more.

How It Works

Step 1
Create Your Account

Register with your username, email, and Indian mobile number. Takes under 30 seconds.

Step 2
Set Your Monthly Budget

Enter your monthly allowance or income. The dashboard tracks your spend against it in real-time.

Step 3
Log Expenses

Add transactions with title, amount, category, date, and currency. AI predicts the category for you.

Step 4
View Insights

Charts, heatmaps, ML analysis, and spending summaries help you understand your habits.

Step 5
Stay in Control

Use the blog, split bills, track mess payments, and hit your savings targets.

Pro Tip: Log expenses daily, even small ones. Students who track consistently report saving 15–20% more each month.
